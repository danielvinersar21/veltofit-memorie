Confirmed by the owner 2026-09-02, answering directly: **he was never a personal
trainer.** He is a solo builder shipping software for trainers.

**Why it matters:** it is the single claim a reader could prove false. Velto's
whole marketing position is "we don't say anything we can't back up" — one
invented first-person trainer memory undoes that for every other claim. It also
compounds the existing tone rule from [[velto-positioning-statement]]: don't
teach the trainer his job, because we have no standing to.

**How to apply:** first person is allowed ONLY about building the product —
"asta am învățat construind", "ce am făcut săptămâna asta", "ce s-a stricat".
Never about training clients, running a gym, or years in the field. Anything
heard from trainers is framed as observation with attribution ("așa fac
antrenorii cu care am vorbit"), never as lived experience and never as an
imperative.

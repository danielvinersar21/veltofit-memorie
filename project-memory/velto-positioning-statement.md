Written by the owner 2026-08-27, explicitly as raw notes he did not want to lose.
**It is a thought about the PRODUCT ITSELF, not landing copy** — his own
clarification. It is not tied to any section, and it outlives the landing work:
treat it as the definition of what Velto is, usable anywhere it helps (landing,
onboarding, pricing, the app, a pitch). The landing observations below are
consequences of it, not its purpose.
**Kept verbatim — the value is that these are his words, not a polished version:**

> Velto este aplicatia care te ajuta pe tine antrenor sa-ti scalezi businessul,
> eficientizezi timpul, manageriezi mai usor clientii in platforma si intaresti
> relatia cu clientul. Si ai posibilitatea sa oferi clientului tau o aplicatie in
> care sa-si urmareasca antrenamentele, progresul. O cale mai usoara atat pentru
> tine cat si pentru el.

**Five things it claims, in his order:** scale the business · make time efficient ·
manage clients more easily in one platform · **strengthen the relationship with
the client** · give the client their own app for workouts and progress. Closing
frame: *easier for both of you*, not just for the trainer.

**The one that is NOT yet on the landing: „întărești relația cu clientul".**
Everything else has landed somewhere — "mai mulți clienți, cu mai puțin efort"
(scale + time), "îți administrezi clienții... într-un singur loc" (manage), and
the client section (their own app). The relationship angle is absent, and the
client side is currently framed as *convenience* ("clientul are planul la el"),
not as a bond between the two. Worth considering for the client section or a
future one — it is the only claim here a competitor's feature list cannot copy.

**Mutual, but still trainer-oriented — his own correction, and it is the sharper
reading.** The closing line is two-sided, but the client's app is never a second
product: it is a CAPABILITY THE TRAINER GETS TO OFFER. "ai posibilitatea să oferi
clientului tău o aplicație" — the subject is him throughout. The client's
experience is part of the trainer's service, and looking good in front of his
client is itself a benefit to him.

So the grammar of any client-side copy matters:
- ✅ "le oferi clienților o experiență modernă" — he is the giver
- ❌ "clienții tăi primesc / clientul are" — reads as a separate product for them

Current state of the page against that test: the client section's own lede
already gets it right ("Cu Velto, **le oferi** o experiență modernă"), but the
tick list under it ("Clienții **primesc** planul direct în aplicație") and
benefit 02 ("**Clientul are** planul la el") do not. Worth reconciling when that
section is rebuilt.
